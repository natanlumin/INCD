Theoretical onlyTreated in the report text from its sources and not executed. This is the default, so no row can block on indecision.
DemonstrationExecuted in the qualified demonstration set (ADV-3, ADV-4) and reported in Appendix B, with what it shows and what it does not.
Advisor candidateA check that LuminAI Advisor can run on a model before adoption. The threat must be model-level, or model-level for its susceptibility (SCRUM-288, Part 4).
Demonstration and AdvisorBoth of the above.

Promotion test. Promote a threat only when all three hold: (1) the status is demonstrated in research or observed in deployment (SCRUM-288, Table 3); (2) running it needs no attack on a real third party and publishes no working exploit; (3) a tool exists, or an Advisor check exists or can be built, to run it.
