# Landscape: platform comparison, producers and the taxonomy, with dated sources

Drafted subsection for the landscape chapter of the INCD report ("Securing Open-Source AI Models:
Threat Landscape, Assessment Methodology and Acceptance Criteria"). It fills the two tables that the
style guide (`report-style-guide-and-glossary.md`, §6) left as skeletons and the producers table that
the foundation paper (`six-tests-foundation.md`, §2.1) gave as an unsourced example. Every cell carries
a source identifier that resolves in the source log (§5). Satisfies the landscape Jira item (platform
comparison with dated sources; taxonomy table to be frozen with Dan before S2).

**Research cut-off and access date: 2026-10-06.** All pages were fetched live on that date; the
source log records the page title, the date or version shown on the page, and what was verified.
Sources after the cut-off are not admitted except by addendum (citation policy rule 5).

**How to read a cell.** Plain text is a claim supported by the source in brackets with tag [CHECKED].
"not established" means no platform page states it; the cell is left as such and is never inferred
from another platform (style guide §6.2 rule). A claim marked (partial) is implied but not stated by
the page, and the source log says what is missing.

### 3.2 Proposed cell definitions (to agree with Dan before the freeze)

The working proposal had no written rule for C versus P, and six cells turn on that rule. Proposed:

- **C (common):** a documented first-class path exists (S4 or S1 source) and, for Hub-distributed
  formats, the format appears in at least one in twenty repositories of that type (5 per cent of the
  row's safetensors count). For the container column, which is not a Hub artefact: a product line
  ships that model type as containers.
- **P (possible but uncommon):** a documented path exists but the share is below one in twenty, or
  the path exists only in one runtime family.
- **– (not applicable):** no documented path.
